Privacy Policy

SMITE 2 Conquest Tracker Discord Bot

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy describes the intended data practices of the community-run SMITE 2 Conquest Tracker bot. The operator should verify and update it to match the deployed bot before public use.

1. Information collected

When used, the bot may process Discord server and user identifiers, player names entered by authorized users, match results, kills, deaths, assists, damage and other manually entered game statistics, ratings, rankings, and MVP counts. Discord may also provide limited interaction metadata needed to respond to slash commands. The bot is not designed to collect passwords, payment information, private messages, or precise location.

2. How information is used

Information is used to record matches, calculate performance scores and season averages, maintain leaderboards, identify players, and respond to Discord commands.

3. Storage and access

Match and player information is intended to be stored in the bot's application database. Authorized server members may see match results and leaderboards through bot commands. The bot operator and anyone with access to the hosting environment or database may be able to access stored data. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

4. Sharing

The bot is not intended to sell personal information or share it with advertisers. Data is processed by Discord when commands are used and may be processed by the operator's hosting provider to run the service. Publicly displayed rankings may be visible to members of the server where commands are used.

5. Retention and deletion

Match records are intended to remain stored until removed by the operator or until the bot's data is reset. To request correction or deletion, contact the server administrator or bot operator through the Discord server where the bot is installed. The operator should handle requests within a reasonable period and document the available deletion procedure.

6. Security

The operator should restrict access to the bot token and database and apply reasonable technical safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The bot is intended for use only by people permitted to use Discord under Discord's terms and applicable law. Users should not submit sensitive information about children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the bot operator or a server administrator in the Discord server where the bot is installed. The operator should add a reliable direct contact method before distributing the bot beyond their own server.

This bot is an independent community project and is not affiliated with Discord, Hi-Rez Studios, or Titan Forge Games.
